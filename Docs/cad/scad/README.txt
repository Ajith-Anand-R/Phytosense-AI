PhytoHydra v1 - mechanical CAD package (prototype, rev A, 2026-10-07)

/scad   Parametric OpenSCAD sources (true solids with holes and pockets). Edit
        trunk_d in phytohydra_params.scad for each palm, press F6, export STL/3MF.
        FreeCAD can import these through its OpenSCAD workbench and export STEP.
/stl    Visual and fit-check meshes: full assembly on a 170 mm trunk, stem band,
        electrode pod, acoustic sentinel, exploded views, edge hub with solar arm.
/render Shaded renders and the dimensioned drawing sheet.

Print settings (suggested): ASA or PA12 (UV and heat resistant), 4 perimeters,
40% gyroid. Straps: TPU 95A. Seal pod and sentinel covers with silicone gasket
or neutral-cure silicone. Electrode pins: titanium grade 2 or SS316, 1.0 mm.
Status: design intent for the prototype; not yet printed or field tested.
